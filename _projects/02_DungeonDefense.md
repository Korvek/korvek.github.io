### A mobile tower defense game developed in Unity 6 as a graduation project at the School of Games. 
![Gameplay Screenshot 1](/assets/images/DungeonDefense/02.png)

### Heroes are invading the dungeon, and players must hold them off for as long as possible. By building multiple layers, each guarded by different monsters, players create a gauntlet that wears the heroes down. When things get tense, they can intervene directly with spells like fireball and freeze.
